## What v0.2.0 is

The second release of Pandokos, theme **Governance**, the R2 sentence of
[requirements §4](../requirements/README.md): enforce rate limits and budgets, sign in to the
administration API with a local account or an external identity provider, manage roles, read reports,
delete a tenant cleanly.

Relative to [v0.1.0](v0.1.0.md): calls are refused before they reach a provider when they would pass
a limit; people sign in to the administration API and act through roles; a platform administrator
enters a tenant only through a recorded support action; a tenant keeps price lists of its own. Reports,
tenant deletion and key rotation are the part of R2 that is still to come (epic 6).

**It is a pre-1.0 release with breaking changes** (next section). Before 1.0 a minor version may break
compatibility, and only the latest minor version is supported.

## Breaking changes compared with v0.1

Read this section before upgrading. Each item says what an operator or an integrator must do.

1. **`suspendTenant` and `reactivateTenant` require a `reason`.** It was optional. A client that sends
   none gets `400 invalid_request` on `reason`. *Do:* send a reason; the CLI commands `tenant suspend`
   and `tenant reactivate` need `--reason`. Seven other operations require one too, but they are new
   ([roles guide](../operations/roles.md#8-the-nine-operations-that-need-a-reason)).
2. **`tenant_setting:write` sets the time zone and the active currency.** Until now `limit:write`
   changed the tenant's time zone and `price_list:write` its active currency. A key scoped to one of
   those and not to the new permission **loses the two settings**. *Do:* grant `tenant_setting:write`
   to the service accounts or roles that set them ([roles guide](../operations/roles.md#3-splitting-the-two-tenant-settings)).
3. **The first service account of a tenant is `owner`, not `tenant-admin`**, and migration 0014 gives
   `owner` to every service account and member that holds `tenant-admin` by an administrator's hand
   (they keep `tenant-admin`; rows written by group rules are left to the rules). *Do:* nothing for
   existing administrators; review who is `owner`, because `owner` alone can assign it
   ([roles guide](../operations/roles.md#1-the-ten-roles)).
4. **A replayed create answers without the one-time secret.** Create operations take an idempotency
   key (`Idempotency-Key`); a retry with the same key and body answers with the stored status and
   body, header `Idempotency-Replayed: true`, and **without a member that is shown once** (a key
   secret, a generated password). Those members are now optional in the response schemas. *Do:* a
   generated client must treat them as optional; a client that retries after a lost response learns the
   resource's ID and revokes or resets it if it never saw the secret ([sign-in guide](../operations/sign-in.md#12-idempotency-keys)).
5. **`TenantCreated.admin_key` may be absent.** The same rule applied to `createTenant`: on a replay
   the first admin key is not repeated. *Do:* handle the absent member.
6. **Operational logs and spans no longer carry tenant fields** ([ADR 0055](../adr/0055-operational-telemetry-holds-no-tenant-data.md),
   [telemetry guide](../operations/telemetry.md)). The request line and the spans name no tenant,
   service account, key, model, size, usage, cost, currency or limit rule; the ledger writer logs no
   row, and a record that cannot be written at shutdown is lost and counted. No setting brings the fields
   back. *Do:* dashboards, alerts and runbooks that group or filter log lines by tenant or model must
   change; investigate through the `request_id` that the tenant has (response header
   `X-Pandokos-Request-Id`, a column of its usage records) and through the tenant's own tools.
7. **Budgets and cost ceilings apply only in the tenant's active currency**, which is
   `pricing.default_currency` (`USD`) for every tenant at the upgrade, and a catalogue price in another
   currency no longer prices the tenant's requests ([database guide](../operations/database.md#17-grants-of-migration-0012)).
   *Do:* an installation whose prices and budgets are in another currency sets
   `pricing.default_currency` **before** starting the new binary, otherwise its budgets are inactive
   until each tenant changes its currency.
8. **Migrations 0011 to 0014 do not run next to the old binary** (stop, migrate, start, see
   "Upgrade notes"), and migration 0011 deletes limit rules that matched nothing (subject gone, key
   revoked, provider or model naming nothing), each with an audit row.

9. **The SDK's own error line is `telemetry error`.** Errors of the OpenTelemetry SDK that belong to
   no single signal were logged as `trace export failing`. They are now `telemetry error`;
   `trace export failing` means a failed export of spans, and the new `metric export failing` a failed
   export of metrics ([telemetry guide](../operations/telemetry.md#7-when-the-collector-is-away),
   [log reference](../reference/logs.md)). *Do:* alerts and filters on the log text `trace export
   failing` that meant "the SDK reported an error" must also match `telemetry error`.

Everything else of the administration API is an addition (new operations, the problem codes
`escalation_refused`, `support_disabled` and `role_in_use`, the member `permissions` of `GET /v1/me`,
the optional `role` of `createPlatformKey`). `info.version` of `api/openapi/admin.v1.yaml` moves to the
release at the final step.

## What is in

By epic, as recorded in `docs/history/`.

### Epic 5, limits and budgets ([record](../history/epic-05-limits-and-budgets/README.md); [limits guide](../operations/limits.md))

- **Limits and reserve and settle** (5.1): rules limit requests per minute, tokens per minute and
  requests in flight for a tenant, a service account or a key; the pipeline reserves an estimate after
  the model check and settles with the usage record's own figures. A refusal is `429 rate_limited`
  with `Retry-After` and limit headers, and leaves a `rejected_limit` usage record. What is strict and
  what is bounded is stated and proven by property and parallel-load tests. Platform ceilings give
  every tenant a default.
- **Quotas and budgets** (5.2): cumulative quotas of requests, tokens and cost per day or month in a
  configured time zone, optionally per provider or model; monetary budgets with reservation
  (`429 quota_exceeded`, `409 no_price` when a budget cannot price a model); soft thresholds and
  exhaustion recorded as limit events; counters rebuilt from the ledger, so a restart does not reset a
  budget.
- **Integrity of limit rules** (5.3): rules tied to their subject and scope by foreign keys, deleted
  with an audit record and a cause when the subject goes, deletion of a referenced provider or model
  refused; a time zone per tenant; unlimited traffic marked in the usage record and traced once a day
  per tenant in the audit log.
- **Tenant price lists** (5.4): price versions per tenant, one list per currency, an active currency
  chosen by the tenant, costs and budgets in that currency, the price source (`catalogue` or `tenant`)
  in the usage record. Usage, limit events and price lists are the tenant's: the platform reads none
  ([ADR 0054](../adr/0054-usage-data-is-the-tenants.md)).

ADRs [0048](../adr/0048-reserve-and-settle.md) to [0054](../adr/0054-usage-data-is-the-tenants.md)
(see the [ADR index](../adr/README.md) for the exact titles).

### Epic 4, rest ([record](../history/epic-04-identity-and-rbac/README.md); [sign-in guide](../operations/sign-in.md), [roles guide](../operations/roles.md), [tokens guide](../operations/tokens.md))

- **Users, local accounts and sessions** (4.2): users with memberships in several tenants; local
  accounts with Argon2id passwords, throttling, locks and a forced first change; server-side sessions
  carried by a cookie that scripts cannot read, with checks against forgery, accepted only on the
  administration listener; sign-in through an OpenID Connect provider (authorization code with PKCE)
  with group rules; local disable that ends sessions within a documented bound; idempotency keys on
  every create; tokens of trusted issuers on the administration listener (`tokens.admin_listener`);
  host commands `user reset-password` and `user unlock`; `pandokos init --admin-username`
  ([ADR 0056](../adr/0056-users-memberships-and-sessions-as-principals.md),
  [0057](../adr/0057-local-accounts-and-sessions-formats-and-numbers.md),
  [0058](../adr/0058-openid-connect-relying-party.md),
  [0059](../adr/0059-idempotency-keys.md)).
- **Roles** (4.3): ten built-in roles pinned by a golden file; custom roles that a tenant writes from
  the permission catalogue; the rule that nobody hands out a permission they do not hold
  (`escalation_refused`); a role in use cannot be deleted (`role_in_use`); platform roles apart from
  tenant roles; **support actions**, the one way a platform administrator acts inside a tenant, with a
  required reason, a bounded time, a record in the tenant's log and a switch (`support.enabled`),
  which is also how a tenant that lost its administrators is recovered; nine operations that need a
  reason; `GET /v1/me`, which reports the effective permissions per tenant; the generated
  [permission reference](../reference/permissions.md) ([ADR 0060](../adr/0060-built-in-roles-custom-roles-and-the-rule-against-escalation.md),
  [0061](../adr/0061-support-actions-and-reasons-how-the-platform-acts-inside-a-tenant.md)). No
  platform permission reads usage, limit events or price lists, even under a support action.

### Epic 6, observability, reports and lifecycle

**Sprint 6.1, metrics and conventions** ([telemetry guide](../operations/telemetry.md), [metrics reference](../reference/metrics.md), [log reference](../reference/logs.md); [ADR 0062](../adr/0062-metrics-instruments-attributes-cardinality-and-scrape.md), [ADR 0063](../adr/0063-the-log-redaction-and-the-log-reference.md)):

- **Metrics over OTLP and for Prometheus.** 41 instruments: requests, latency, time to first chunk,
  tokens, estimated tokens, cost, limit refusals and provider 429 answers, and the state of the ledger,
  authentication, issuer keys, sign-in, limits and the telemetry itself. The GenAI and HTTP metrics follow
  the OpenTelemetry semantic conventions **1.41.0**, pinned (the last version whose Go package carries
  the GenAI conventions; a change will be announced here). They are exported to the collector of the
  traces, and served at `GET /metrics` on the administration listener when asked.
- **Three new keys**, all optional: `telemetry.metrics.otlp` (default `true`),
  `telemetry.metrics.export_interval` (default `60s`, 5 s to 10 min) and `telemetry.metrics.prometheus`
  (default `false`), with the environment variables `PANDOKOS_TELEMETRY_METRICS_OTLP`,
  `PANDOKOS_TELEMETRY_METRICS_EXPORT_INTERVAL` and `PANDOKOS_TELEMETRY_METRICS_PROMETHEUS`. With a
  collector set, **metrics are exported from the upgrade on** unless `telemetry.metrics.otlp` is false.
- **A new permission, `metrics:read`**, a platform permission held by `platform-admin` and
  `platform-auditor`. The scrape endpoint is never anonymous: a scraper presents a platform key and the
  recommended one is `pandokos platform-key create --role platform-auditor`. Platform keys carry no
  scopes.
- **No tenant in a metric.** No metric names a tenant, a caller, a key, a request or a trace, and no
  setting adds one; the opt-in tenant dimension of ADR 0015 is withdrawn (issue #52 designs a
  pseudonymised one). The one identifier is the provider credential ID on the counter of the provider's
  429 answers. A model or a currency used by one tenant alone makes its total that tenant's.
- **The log.** One redacting handler for every logger, which also redacts values that have the shape of
  a Pandokos secret (a key, a session token, a bearer value, a compact JWS) and counts them; every
  message and field is in the [log reference](../reference/logs.md), kept in step by tests. The SDK's
  own error line is renamed `telemetry error` (breaking change 9).
- **An observability overlay** for the Compose reference deployment: a collector, Prometheus and
  Grafana with two dashboards (`docker compose -f compose.yaml -f observability/compose.yaml up -d
  --wait`, [Compose guide](../operations/compose.md#the-observability-overlay)). `setup.sh` also creates
  `secrets/grafana_admin`. An example, not a hardened monitoring stack.

Sprints 6.2 and 6.3 are not delivered. Their sections are written when they are.

## Known limits

From the records of epics 4 and 5; the limits of [v0.1.0](v0.1.0.md#known-limits) that these epics do
not lift still hold. To be revised after epic 6.

- **No multi-factor authentication**, recovery by e-mail, self-registration, password history or social
  login (excluded by [ADR 0022](../adr/0022-local-accounts-and-sessions.md)). A local account has one
  factor and the throttle can lock a known username out: prefer an identity provider that enforces
  your policy, or keep the administration listener on a private network ([sign-in guide](../operations/sign-in.md#13-what-is-not-built)).
- **No check of passwords against breached-password lists** (sprint 10.2).
- **Pending platform-issued password gap, until sprint 10.2**: a membership or role gained while a
  platform-issued password is still pending (until changed or expired) is not checked against the
  caller's permissions. Accepted by the maintainer as a residual risk.
- **`updatePlatformIssuer` is not guarded**: a platform administrator can change what a platform-level
  issuer trusts and act as the service accounts that tenants mapped under it. Accepted as a residual
  risk ([ADR 0061](../adr/0061-support-actions-and-reasons-how-the-platform-acts-inside-a-tenant.md));
  the change is in the platform's audit log.
- Provider-initiated logout, refresh tokens and step-up authentication (IAM-17) are not built; one
  identity provider for the installation, none per tenant; no bearer token that acts as a user; a user
  cannot be deleted yet (sprint 6.3); the binary serves no page.
- Roles: no role hierarchies, deny rules, conditions on a permission or permissions on single
  resources; no custom platform roles; no scopes per subject mapping; no guard that keeps the last
  owner (a tenant without an owner is administered by `tenant-admin` and an owner is appointed through
  a support action).
- **Limit counters are per gateway instance**; several instances each enforce alone until epic 8
  (shared counters). A request with budget limits is more expensive on the hot path (about 105
  allocations per reserve and settle) than one with rate limits only.
- Limit events and unlimited-traffic traces are not delivered to other systems (sprint 6.4, release
  v0.3.0); limit metrics, a "used against limit" report and usage rollups are sprints 6.1 and 6.2.
- No platform aggregates per tenant, by design: usage is the tenant's.
- Operators cannot group log lines or metrics by tenant until a pseudonymised tenant dimension exists
  (issue #52).
- The GenAI conventions are in Development. The pinned version 1.41.0 is the last that the Go
  semantic-convention packages carry; the source of the names must be found again for a later one.
- Records lost at shutdown cannot be rebuilt from the log.

## Upgrade notes

From v0.1.x (schema of migrations 0001 to 0008):

1. **Read "Breaking changes" above**, and set `pricing.default_currency` first if your prices are not
   in `USD`.
2. **Migrations 0009 to 0014** are applied by `pandokos migrate` (files in
   `internal/store/migrations/sql`; per-migration notes in the [database guide](../operations/database.md)):

   | Migration | Adds |
   |---|---|
   | `0009_limit_rules` | limit rules (5.1) |
   | `0010_quotas_budgets` | quotas, budgets, limit events (5.2) |
   | `0011_limit_integrity` | rules tied to subjects and scopes by foreign keys (5.3) |
   | `0012_tenant_price_lists` | price lists, active currency (5.4) |
   | `0013_users_sessions` | users, memberships, sessions, idempotency keys (4.2) |
   | `0014_roles_support` | custom roles, support actions, the role `owner` (4.3) |

   **Stop every instance, run `pandokos migrate`, start the new binary.** Migrations 0011 to 0014 are
   not compatible with the earlier binaries (they turn not ready and refuse requests), and a new
   binary does not start against an older schema. There is no down migration: take a backup before
   and restore it to go back. 0009 and 0010 alone would have been safe before the new binary.
3. **Create the first person**: `pandokos init --admin-username NAME` (an existing installation keeps
   its keys). After the upgrade `serve` warns while local accounts are on and no active user holds a
   platform role; or link a person through the identity provider.
4. **No rule means no limit**: nothing is refused until a rule is written. Write rules with the CLI or
   the administration API ([limits guide](../operations/limits.md#13-managing-rules)). With budgets, a
   model without a price in the budget's currency is refused (`no_price`): add a price or set
   `limits.on_unknown_price` to `allow`.
5. **New configuration keys**, all optional ([configuration reference](../operations/configuration.md)):
   - `telemetry.metrics.otlp`, `telemetry.metrics.export_interval`, `telemetry.metrics.prometheus`
     (sprint 6.1); the permission `metrics:read` needs no migration;
   - `auth.*`: local accounts (`auth.local.*`), sessions (`auth.session.*`), `auth.public_origin`
     (set it when the listener has no TLS outside loopback) and OpenID Connect with group rules
     (`auth.oidc.*`);
   - `support.enabled` (`true`) and `support.max_duration` (`1h`, at most 24 h);
   - `idempotency.retention` (`24h`);
   - `tokens.admin_listener` (`false`);
   - limits: `limits.default_output_tokens`, `limits.time_zone` (`UTC`), `limits.on_unknown_price`
     (`refuse`), `limits.on_store_failure` (`refuse`), `limits.reconcile_interval` (`5m`),
     `cache.limits_ttl` (`30s`); and `pricing.default_currency` (`USD`). Set `limits.time_zone` and
     `pricing.default_currency` identically on every instance.
6. **Roles and tenants' administrators**: see breaking changes 2 and 3; support actions are on by
   default (`support.enabled`), which is what recovers a tenant without administrators.
